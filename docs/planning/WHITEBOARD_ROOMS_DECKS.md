# Whiteboard — Rooms, Decks, and Channel Pockets (future)

**Purpose:** Document the future of literal spatial context on the whiteboard so the ship and subagents/subtasks complete in their own contextual and conceptual spaces without overbearing architect task loads and system collapse/failure.

---

## Vision

The whiteboard is for the **entirety of Station Calyx** as we test. Eventually we must be able to draw **literal "rooms"** for Station Calyx (or, e.g., different **ship decks** for the pirate theme — graphic design later) and **channel pocket sized containers** for some of the tasks we take on. This ensures:

- The **ship** and **subagents / subtasks** can complete in their own **contextual and conceptual spaces**.
- Architect (CBO) task load is **not overbearing** — work is distributed across spaces and pockets.
- We avoid **system collapse / failure** from a single overloaded context.

---

## Concepts

- **Rooms or ship decks:** Drawable, distinct conceptual spaces (e.g. Bridge, Engine room, Comms). Each space has its own context and optional assignees. Subagents and subtasks run in their space so CBO is not the only bottleneck.
- **Channel pocket containers:** Small, bounded containers for specific tasks or channels. Tasks live in a pocket (a room/deck or a channel); completion and failure are scoped to that pocket so the whole station doesn’t go down.

---

## Design note

Literal rooms/decks and pocket containers support a **live and attentive designing experience**: keyboard and pointer input on the whiteboard interact with Station Calyx and Calyx Agents in bounded spaces. Graphic design (literal ship decks, pirate aesthetic) we come back to later.
