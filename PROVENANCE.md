# Project Provenance

Station Calyx / AI-For-All is maintained by [Narth](https://github.com/Narth).

The repository has been developed through sustained human–AI collaboration. Commits and documents may include work produced with ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, local models, and named Station collaborators such as CBO. Where available, commit authorship, co-author trailers, pull request discussion, receipts, and runtime evidence provide a more precise record than narrative attribution alone.

## Authority and authorship are different

An AI tool may draft prose, code, tests, or analysis. That contribution does not give the tool ownership of the repository, human identity, governance authority, or permission to publish. Maintainer review and explicit operator authorization remain distinct from generation.

Likewise, human authorship does not make a technical claim true. Claims about implementation and operation should be checked against current code, tests, and evidence.

## Persona and narrative

Station Calyx uses names, roles, ceremonies, and narrative language to make a complex collaboration understandable. Those elements express project culture; they are not proof of consciousness, legal personhood, uninterrupted identity, or runtime capability.

CBO is presented as a bounded steward and collaborator. Human authority remains primary.

## Historical material

The repository contains dated reports, experiments, plans, patches, and archived systems from multiple phases of the project. Their presence preserves learning and provenance but does not make them current guidance or canonical runtime authority.
